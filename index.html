Theory & comparison

Regular languages are closed under all four operations. Union, intersection and difference use the product construction: states are pairs (qA, qB), start state is (startA, startB), and δ((p,q),a) = (δA(p,a), δB(q,a)). Only reachable pairs are generated (BFS). Complement flips accepting states of a complete DFA and is taken relative to Σ*.
